# Off-page and mentions plan

## The honest position

The domain is days old with, as far as I can verify, no referring domains. I have
no backlink export, so that is an observation from the domain age rather than a
measurement. Nothing here promises a ranking; link acquisition changes the
probability, over months.

No paid links, no exchanges, no directories that exist only to sell links. Those
carry a real penalty risk and would waste the one asset this site has, which is
that its claims check out.

## The asset you already have, and nobody else does

Every entry shows its grading. The competitor currently winning "dua for anxiety"
shows none, and cites a source on one of its thirteen duʿās. That gap is the
pitch for every item below. You are not asking anyone to link to another duʿā
list. You are asking them to link to the one where the sourcing can be checked.

## Targets

| Source type | Specific angle | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Islamic studies departments, university chaplaincies | A free, verified, ad-free reference with gradings shown. Chaplaincy pages link to resources for students. | M |
| Masjid and community websites | Same, framed as something to link from a "resources" page. Start with masjids you have a real connection to. | L |
| Muslim mental health practitioners and counsellors | The anxiety, grief, despair and distress hubs, as something to give a client that is not a random blog. | M |
| Hadith and Qurʾan app developers | The verification method itself. Developers link to sources they use or admire. | M |
| Islamic subreddits and forums, where relevant and not spammed | Only in threads asking whether a specific duʿā is authentic, answering the question and citing the entry. | L |
| Fact-check and myth-correction angle | The "commonly shared but not authentic" article from the content plan. This is the linkable asset. | H |
| Unlinked mentions | Once the site is known, search the brand and request a link where mentioned. Not applicable yet. | L |

## The one asset worth building

Article 12 in the content plan, the list of widely circulated duʿās that fail the
grading rule, with each grader named and the refusal shown. It is the only thing
on this list that people link to without being asked, because it answers a
question everyone in the niche has and nobody answers with evidence.

## What not to do

Do not buy links. Do not submit to bulk directories. Do not write guest posts for
sites that exist to host guest posts. Do not ask for links before the site is
indexed; there is nothing for the link to pass value to yet.
