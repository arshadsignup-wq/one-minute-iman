# Content plan: oneminuteiman.xyz

## The recommendation first: do not start a blog yet

The obvious move is a blog, and it is the wrong one for this site right now.

You already have three layers competing for the same queries: 43 situation hubs,
3,371 entry pages, and the Qurʾan and hadith browsers. A fourth layer of articles
would target the same terms as the hubs and split them. The skill's Pattern E
calls this cannibalisation, and it is the most common way a site with real depth
makes itself weaker.

The hubs are now the right format for those queries: keyword H1, direct answer,
FAQ, and 40 to 130 verified entries underneath. Nothing an article could do for
"dua for anxiety" that the hub cannot now do better.

**Write articles only where no hub can serve the query.** Those are the twelve below.

## Why these and not "10 duas for stress"

Numbers 8 and 12 are the two that matter most and no competitor is doing them.
The site's whole differentiator is that it grades everything and refuses what
fails. An article that shows a widely shared duʿā being refused, with the
graders named, is the single most linkable and most quotable thing this site
could publish. It is also the thing an AI answer engine will cite, because it
answers a question nobody else answers with evidence.

## Format standard for each

Direct answer in the first 60 words. Question-shaped H2s. The Arabic, the
transliteration, the translation and the grading for every text named. Internal
link up to the hub and out to the individual entries. No dashes. Author byline
once there is a named author, which there is not yet.

## Before any of this

None of it matters until Search Console is verified and the sitemap submitted.
Publishing more pages into an unindexed site does nothing.
