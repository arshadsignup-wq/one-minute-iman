What you should do first

Everything below is already built and deployed. One thing is not, and cannot be, done by me.

Verify Search Console and submit the sitemap

Go to Google Search Console, verify www.oneminuteiman.xyz, and submit /sitemap.xml. Until that happens, none of the work in this report can produce a single visit. It needs your Google account, so it is the one action I could not take for you.

Three root causes were found. All three have been addressed in code and deployed, except the parts that require facts only you hold. The site went from having no sitemap, no robots file, no canonical tags, no social cards and no structured data, to having all of them, plus 43 rebuilt landing pages and an About page.

10

Appendix: what the review changed

Two critique passes were run against this work before delivery.

  • Pass 1 flagged that 116 hadith citations had been written but not checked. All were then verified against the site's data. Zero were wrong.
  • Pass 2 overturned a call made in pass 1. Two hubs, feeling overwhelmed and severe distress, had been judged as separate intents sharing a source. The evidence said otherwise: identical headline supplication, identical top entries, near identical descriptions. That is a duplicate. The overwhelmed hub was rewritten to lead with Qurʾan 2:286, which is tagged to that hub alone.
  • Four of my own errors were caught by the automated pass: two titles over 60 characters, one description over 155, and a dash surviving in the refused entries data.
